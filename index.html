Stone Sentinel Combat

In Wizard's Chess, pieces are living ancient stone golems. When an enemy piece is captured, the attacker draws their weapon and violently shatters the defender into flying stone rubble and ash!

Recreate the legendary endgame from Harry Potter and the Sorcerer's Stone. Play as Ronald Weasley upon the White Knight to deliver check, lure the Queen away, and allow Harry Potter (White Bishop) to checkmate the Dark King!
